Frequently Asked Questions

What is blockchain asset management? +

It is the process of auditing, tracking, and allocating digital assets across cryptographic chains using read-only API logs, address observers, and index calculations.

How can I monitor my portfolio? +

You can connect public wallet addresses. Stackly scans ledger histories and updates your balances automatically in the console.
